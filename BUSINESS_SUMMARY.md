# Business Summary

Breakout Radar is a private A&R research workspace for a record label focused on emerging African artists.

It brings artist discovery, sourced audience observations, human artistic review, growth-integrity investigation, and scouting workflow into one place. Staff can find an artist through MusicBrainz, confirm the identity, add the artist to the radar, attach authorized analytics exports, compare compatible aggregate audience snapshots, record evidence-based reviews, and track next steps through the pipeline.

The platform is designed to improve the quality and auditability of signing and development decisions. It distinguishes observed facts from hypotheses, records reviewer and decision history, and leaves unsupported metrics unavailable instead of inventing them. A signing remains a human decision.

Current limits: this is not yet a production-verified service. Supabase migrations and permissions still need staging validation; no licensed audience or UGC provider is connected; automated ingestion is not scheduled; and there is no trained model or validated breakout backtest. The system can support a governed internal research workflow once the label configures Supabase, approves data sources, and validates access controls.
